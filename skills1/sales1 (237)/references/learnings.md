# IdeaProof — Learnings

Accumulated platform knowledge. Append new findings with a dated line.

**2026-07-05**: Research baseline. IdeaProof (ideaproof.io) is an AI startup-idea validation +
pre-launch suite ("test your idea in 120s"). Multi-model ensemble (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok) + live
web scraping. Credit-based, no subscription: free 90 credits (≈ ~2 full validations, not the "4"
marketed); packs €19.99–99.99 (Starter/Builder/Founder); credits never expire. Modules: validation
(viability score + GO/NO-GO, ~40 cr), market/competitor analysis (~100), business plan (~100), brand
strategy w/ 12 Jungian archetypes (~50), logo/visual identity (~150), marketing/ad suite (~250), pitch
deck (~120).

**2026-07-05**: **No documented public API, no webhooks, no Zapier/Make.** UI-only tool. The advertised
OpenRouter integration is internal model routing, not a customer-facing API. Get data out by
downloading/copying manually.

**2026-07-05**: Independent-review criticism (preuve.ai, worthbuild.io comparisons): viability score
runs high (median ~78/100 — "feel-good machine"); "89% accuracy" claim has no published methodology;
"50+ sources" but report footers cite only ~5; zero third-party reviews on G2/Trustpilot/Capterra;
free-tier credit math misleading. "A tool doing six jobs rarely goes as deep on any one." → Positioned
in the skill as a directional gut check + starter-asset generator, with the go/no-go pushed to a real
demand test via /sales-idea-validation.

**2026-07-05**: Homepage (ideaproof.io) fetched fine (not JS-blocked). Pricing/feature detail also
corroborated via preuve.ai/compare/ideaproof and worthbuild.io comparison blog.

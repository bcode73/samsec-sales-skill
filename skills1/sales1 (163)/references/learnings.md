**2026-06-13**: API re-verified against live official docs — significant drift found. Endpoint paths corrected: reverse email lookup is `/v1/api/person` (+`in_realtime` param), email finder `GET /v1/api/find-email` (`fullName`+`companyDomain`, was POST), phone finder `GET /v1/api/mobile-finder` (`linkedin_profile`, async/202, was POST), company lookup `/v1/api/company` (was company-lookup), IP-to-company `/v1/api/ip-to-company-lookup` (was ip-to-company), email validation `/v1/api/verify-email` (was email-validation), reverse-email-append `/v1/api/reverse-hash-lookup`, linkedin-to-email is v2 `GET /v2/api/linkedin-to-email`. Rate limits ARE now documented: 300 req/min, burst 1500 per 5-min window (was "not publicly documented"). Pricing model changed to credit packs: Growth $49/mo 100K credits, Scale $149/mo 500K, Pro $499/mo 2.5M, PAYG from $20/100; free trial 100 credits; per-endpoint credits email-validation 1 / email-finder 10 / reverse-lookup 10 / phone-finder 500 (old $59/$99 Starter/Professional tiers and 25-credit free are gone). Added: Check Credit Usage endpoint + Search People/Company endpoints. No webhook HMAC signing documented (unchanged).

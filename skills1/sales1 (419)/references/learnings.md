**2026-07-05**: Research baseline — platform positioning, module set, pricing, and the no-API reality captured from third-party sources on this date. ⚠️ The **live site was unreachable**: `plannit.ai` / `aigenerator.com` returned an **expired TLS certificate** and all direct WebFetches failed, and `web.archive.org` was not fetchable either; research was assembled from Upmetrics, AIChief, Trustpilot, Capterra, and comparison articles only. Re-verify specifics against current docs before relying on them.

**2026-07-05**: **Rebrand** — `plannit.ai` now serves **AIGenerator.com**; both are **AIGenerator LLC**. A competitor review (Upmetrics) claims "Plannit is no longer available; their domain redirects to an unreliable third-party site," but AIChief and AIGenerator's own site confirm the business-plan generator is **active in 2026** under the AIGenerator brand. Treat "it's dead" claims as competitor framing.

**2026-07-05**: **Disambiguation** — `plannit.io` is a **different, unrelated app** (home/property/field-services management, with its own help.plannit.io integrations). Do NOT conflate it with the plannit.ai/AIGenerator business-plan tool.

**2026-07-05**: **Pricing model changed with the rebrand.** Old Plannit AI = **$39.99 one-time per plan** (lifetime), with 5/10-plan bundles + Enterprise. New AIGenerator.com = **free tier + ~$19/mo Pro** (advanced financials, unlimited exports). Sources disagree; verify on the live site.

**2026-07-05**: **No public API.** One review said "API is Available at Plannit AI," but that conflated the **internal GPT-4 API** it uses to generate plans — there is no developer-facing API, webhooks, Zapier/Make, or MCP. UI-only, consistent with the whole idea-validation/business-plan cluster.

**2026-07-05**: **Weakest module = financial forecasting** (rated ~3.0/5 on review pages) and **customization ~3.2/5**; top qualitative complaint is that plans **lack depth for niche/specialized industries** and read cookie-cutter. The lever is input richness + heavy editing. Differentiator vs VentureKit: **real-time team collaboration** (Plannit has it, VentureKit doesn't).

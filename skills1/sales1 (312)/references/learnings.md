# ListingBott — Learnings

Accumulated, dated platform knowledge. Append new findings with a date stamp so staleness is auditable.

---

**2026-06-27**: Research baseline. Built from the marketing site (listingbott.com) and review/comparison articles (ColdIQ, Capterra, AI Agents Directory, RafflePress/Woorise/AutoSaaSLaunch comparisons). Active product (founder John Rush; ~1000+ users; beta-priced).

- **Category:** AI-powered **done-for-you directory submission service** (SEO/launch). **Not a tool** — there's **no API, no Zapier, no self-service dashboard**. Communication is an onboarding form + weekly email updates + a final report.
- **What it does:** submits your site to **100+ relevant directories** curated (by goal: DR boost vs brand) from a **10,000+** directory/site/forum database. AI agents scan daily for new directories; AI + humans submit.
- **Process:** gradual **~4-5 week** roll-out, **human-like paced** ("99.9% safe") to dodge spam filters; **approve/decline** review; **automatic duplicate detection**; you **keep each listing's login** and can edit/remove (host sites can also remove per policy).
- **Guarantee:** commonly **DR 0→15 in ~2 months or refund** — meaningful for brand-new domains, modest for established ones. Read current terms.
- **Pricing (beta phase, per website):** ~$299 (early) / ~$399 / ~$499 (current std) / ~$999 (post-beta, +partner-blog backlinks). Bulk discounts for 3+. One-time, not subscription.
- **Audience:** indie makers, bootstrapped + VC startups, YC founders, SaaS/AI/no-code/dev tools, agencies, ecommerce, courses — strong fit for the maker/founder launch GTM.
- **Competitive set:** AutoSaaSLaunch (Chrome ext, ~$29 self-serve), SubmitSaaS (~$60–140 DFY), GetMoreBacklinks (DFY service — has a skill), LaunchDirectories (~$119–219), StartupSubmit (manual DFY), SubmitPro.ai, plus DIY. ListingBott's angle: curated + paced + DR guarantee at a mid/premium price.
- **Caveats to flag:** directory backlinks are a *foundational* SEO signal (not a ranking hack); per-website cost adds up for serial launchers; it's slow by design; verify current guarantee/refund terms before buying a high tier.

⚠️ **Skill shape note:** this is a **no-API service skill** (modeled on `sales-getmorebacklinks`) — no `references/{platform}-api-reference.md` because there is no API. Examples are service/decision-oriented (pay vs DIY, expectations, results), not code recipes.

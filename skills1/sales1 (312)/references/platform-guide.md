## What ListingBott is

An **AI-powered, done-for-you directory submission service** for launching startups (by indie maker John Rush). You hand it your product; it submits you to **100+ relevant directories**, hand-curated from a **10,000+** database of directories/sites/forums. It is a **managed service, not a tool** — there is no dashboard, API, or self-serve UI. Target users: indie makers, bootstrapped + VC-backed startups, YC founders, SaaS/AI tools, no-code/dev tools, agencies, ecommerce, courses, marketplaces.

**It's a service, not software.** Don't look for an API/Zapier/dashboard — communication is an onboarding form in, weekly email updates + a final report out.

## How it works (the process)

1. **Onboarding form** — you submit product details + your goal (DR boost vs brand awareness).
2. **Curation** — ListingBott's AI agents (which scan the web daily for new directories) + humans select ~100 relevant directories from the 10,000+ database for your goal.
3. **Approve/decline** — you can review and decline suggested directories before they're used.
4. **Gradual submission (~4-5 weeks)** — submissions roll out over about a month in a **human-like, paced** pattern (described as "99.9% safe") to avoid spam filters. AI + humans do the submitting.
5. **Duplicate detection** — automatically skips directories you're already listed on.
6. **Weekly updates + final report** — you get email progress (submitted / live statuses) and a final report of all live listings.
7. **You own the listings** — you keep each listing's login and can edit/remove anytime (host sites may also remove per policy).

**Indexing timeline:** individual directories publish/index anywhere from hours to weeks after submission, so DR impact lags the submission window.

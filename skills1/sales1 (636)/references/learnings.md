**2026-07-06**: Research baseline — platform features, tiers, integrations, and the no-public-API reality captured from live sources (upmetrics.co, upmetrics.co/llm-info) plus G2/Capterra/Trustpilot review snippets on this date. Re-verify specifics against current docs before relying on them.

**2026-07-06**: Namesake collision confirmed — upmetrics.**co** is the AI business-planning tool; upmetrics.**com** is an unrelated nonprofit impact/ESG-reporting platform (a different company). Search results mix the two; always check the TLD before quoting features, pricing, or "API" availability.

**2026-07-06**: No public API for the business-planning product. `github.com/upmetrics` exists (blog → upmetrics.co, "Online Business Plan Software!") but is marketing, not a product API. Any "API/white-label" is a custom, sales-gated arrangement for advisors/accelerators — undocumented, not self-serve. UI-only: no webhooks, Zapier/Make, or MCP.

**2026-07-06**: Top review pain points to keep addressing — (1) AI drafts read generic / ~60% rewrite; (2) whole input sections can vanish without warning (save/export often); (3) auto-renewal at full price with weak cancellation confirmation; (4) QuickBooks/Xero is a one-way import, not real-time two-way sync despite "sync" marketing. Support quality is the most consistently praised aspect (G2 support ~9.5/10).

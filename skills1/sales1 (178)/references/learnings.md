**2026-05-31**: Research baseline (from git history) — platform docs were captured on/around this date and the API surface, pricing, and webhooks have NOT been re-verified against live docs since. Re-verify specifics before relying on them.
**2026-06-13**: API/pricing re-verified against live official docs — corrected several drifted pricing claims. SalesRabbit has no $25-35 entry tier (cheapest paid is Team $59/user/mo, Pro $75/$49-annual, Enterprise quote, RoofLink Pro $120). Map My Customers floor is $55 not $40 (tiers $55/$79/$99/$129). Spotio moved to quote-only B2B/B2C/Custom plans (5-rep min, no public prices). Geopointe is now part of Ascent Cloud (geopointe.com 301s to ascentcloud.io) with quote-based pricing — prior ~$55/user/mo no longer published. Outfield pricing page no longer shows dollar amounts. Outscraper is pay-as-you-go ($3/1k records, 500 free) not a $50-200/mo flat sub. Yelp Fusion has no standing free tier — free-trial-then-paid commercial plans. Foursquare Places free tier cut to 500 Pro calls/mo as of Jun 1 2026, then ~$15/1k (Premium endpoints no free tier). Unchanged: Badger Maps $58/$95, Phantombuster $69/mo+, Repsly and Skynamo quote-based, Google Maps Places pay-per-request ($2-30/1k, $200/mo credit). Sources: https://salesrabbit.com/pricing/, https://www.badgermapping.com/pricing/, https://mapmycustomers.com/pricing/, https://spotio.com/pricing/, https://www.ascentcloud.io/pricing, https://www.outfieldapp.com/pricing, https://outscraper.com/outscraper-pricing-explained/, https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/plans, https://docs.foursquare.com/developer/reference/upcoming-changes.

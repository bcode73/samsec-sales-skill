Unchanged: base URL api.planhat.com, analytics.planhat.com, Bearer/Private-App auth, 200/min soft + 150/sec + 50-parallel hard limits, 5,000 bulk items, 32MB/~150k body, MCP at /v1/mcp, quote-based 3-tier pricing + 4 add-ons.

**2026-05-30**: Initial research — IMPORTANT: live domain is `referralkit.co`, NOT `referralkit.io` (the .io domain returns ECONNREFUSED and may be stale/squatted). Built by Copyhackers. Pricing model has shifted — homepage advertises "100% free for your first 10,000 leads" while older third-party sources mention "$19/mo for 3K subscribers" with a 30-day trial. /pricing returned 404 at research time; verify current pricing with support before quoting. Live ESP integrations: Mailchimp, ConvertKit (Kit), MailerLite, AWeber. Planned (not live): Drip, Ghost, Campaign Monitor, ActiveCampaign. No API/webhooks/Zapier/MCP confirmed via WebSearch. Differentiator vs SparkLoop: merge-tag-driven flow with no separate landing pages or JS widgets, plus the generous free tier. The 10K "leads" terminology vs "subscribers" is ambiguous — confirm with support before planning headroom.

**2026-06-13**: API re-verified against live official docs — no drift found. Homepage still states verbatim "100% free for your first 10,000 leads" and "Built with ❤️ by Copyhackers"; live ESP list (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, MailerLite, AWeber) and roadmap list ("On our roadmap we have Drip, Ghost, Campaign Monitor, and Active Campaign") both unchanged; /pricing and /plans still return HTTP 404; no API/webhooks/Zapier surface exists (no ReferralKit app on Zapier, no developer docs). NEW corroboration: the official AWeber partner integration page describes the paid model as a "30-day free trial" including "200 subscribers, unlimited referrals, unlimited rewards, and unlimited team members" — this coexists with the homepage free-10K claim, so the pricing ambiguity the skill flags is real and now confirmed by an official AWeber page, not just older third parties. Sources: https://referralkit.co/, https://www.aweber.com/integrations/other/referralkit/.

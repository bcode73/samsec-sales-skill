**2026-07-08**: Research baseline — platform positioning, modules (quick evaluation, assessment/evaluation points, improve-your-idea, one-time business plan), the "feedback personality" input, pricing (Free ~5 ideas/day + ~160-char output + no customization; Premium ~$5/mo intro → ~$19/mo unlimited + all points + 500+ chars + customizable; Business Plan ~$14.99 one-time, 15–25 pages), and the no-public-API surface captured from live sources on this date. Re-verify specifics against current docs before relying on them.

**2026-07-08**: Domain gotcha — the product is at **rebecc.ai**. `rebeccai.com` (the URL originally in the backlog) is a **parked/for-sale GoDaddy domain**, not the product. Always use rebecc.ai.

**2026-07-08**: No public API / webhooks / Zapier / Make / MCP — UI-only web app. Only the business-plan document exports (edit/save/download). Don't plan an integration around RebeccAi; pull real demand signals from source instead.

**2026-07-08**: Reviewer criticism (ReadySetLaunch /compare, AIBox100) worth surfacing to users — (1) the **feedback-personality toggle lets founders bias scoring** toward a preferred tone (undermines objectivity); (2) business plans are generated for **unvalidated** ideas ("15-page fantasy") with **hallucinated financials** and no real market input; (3) **single text-box input**, no structured questioning or per-gap follow-up; (4) **no verified third-party reviews** on G2/Trustpilot/Capterra.

# Templates

Reusable business templates a reader fills in for their own company: proposal template, customer persona template, follow-up email templates, pricing ladder template, referral request template, and similar. Distinct from worksheets in `/worksheets`: a worksheet is a one-time reflective exercise; a template is a document format the reader reuses indefinitely in their own business.

Populated during Phase 17 (Appendices) and as each relevant module is drafted.

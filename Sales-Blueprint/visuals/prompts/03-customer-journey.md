# Visual Prompt: Customer Journey

**Purpose:** Shows the four stages a buyer moves through internally, independent of the seller, so a reader understands why discovery has to capture more than one moment in time.

**Placement:** Module 4, Learn the Customer, replacing `visuals/diagrams/figure-4.1-customer-journey.mmd`.

**Caption:** Figure 4.1: four stages of a buyer's own journey toward a decision, each needing different information from them.

**Generation prompt:**
A minimal timeline diagram on a warm cream background (#FBF9F4). Four circular nodes arranged in two rows of two, read left to right then down, connected by a thin charcoal line (#1B1F1E) that runs across the first row, drops to the second, then runs across it. Each node a pale teal fill (#E3EEEC) with a deep teal (#0F3D3E) border. Nodes labelled in sentence case, in order: "Aware of the problem," "Evaluating options" (row one); "Deciding internally," "Justifying the choice" (row two). Beneath each node, one short italic sans-serif line of explanatory text (aware: "knows something needs to change"; evaluating: "comparing suppliers, weighing risk"; deciding: "building consensus with the committee"; justifying: "defending it to their board or team"). Clean sans-serif typography, no icons, no gradients, no drop shadows. Portrait or near-square proportions, sized to sit within a single body-text column.

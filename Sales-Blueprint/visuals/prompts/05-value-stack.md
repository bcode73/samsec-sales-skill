# Visual Prompt: Value Stack

**Purpose:** Shows why a price looks small against several itemised outcomes but large against any single one, reinforcing the case for presenting value before price.

**Placement:** Module 6, Establish Value, replacing `visuals/diagrams/figure-6.1-value-stack.mmd`.

**Caption:** Figure 6.1: each layer is a distinct, buyer-confirmed outcome. The price sits at the bottom, visually smaller than the pile of value stacked above it.

**Generation prompt:**
A minimal vertical bar-stack diagram on a warm cream background (#FBF9F4). Three stacked rectangles of equal width but increasing height going upward, each in pale teal (#E3EEEC) with a deep teal (#0F3D3E) border, labelled bottom to top in sentence case: "Recovered focus time," "Avoided cost of a full office expansion," "Improved in-office attendance." Beneath the whole stack, a small, visually narrower charcoal (#1B1F1E) rectangle labelled "The price" in cream text, deliberately drawn smaller in scale than the stack above it to make the visual point that the price looks small against the pile. Clean sans-serif labels, no gradients, no drop shadows, generous whitespace around the composition. Portrait orientation.

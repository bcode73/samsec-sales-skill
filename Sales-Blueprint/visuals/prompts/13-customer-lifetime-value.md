# Visual Prompt: Customer Lifetime Value

**Purpose:** Shows, as a chart rather than a diagram, that a nurtured relationship with systematic referral asks compounds in value over years, while a single transaction stays flat.

**Placement:** Module 11, Turn Customers into Advocates, replacing `visuals/charts/figure-11.2-customer-lifetime-value.mmd`.

**Caption:** Figure 11.2: a single transaction produces one flat line of value. A nurtured, systematically-asked relationship compounds, because referrals bring new customers who can themselves become advocates.

**Generation prompt:**
A minimal line chart on a warm cream background (#FBF9F4), data-visualisation style rather than a process diagram. X-axis labelled "Year" with three points (1, 2, 3); y-axis labelled "Cumulative value," unlabelled numeric scale, faint grid lines in a pale neutral tone. Two lines: a flat charcoal (#1B1F1E) line labelled "Single transaction, no follow-up," staying level across all three years; a rising deep teal (#0F3D3E) line labelled "Nurtured relationship with referral asks," curving upward and diverging clearly from the flat line by year two and three. Emphasise the endpoint of the rising line with a small filled dot. Clean sans-serif axis labels and legend, no gradients, no drop shadows, no decorative elements beyond the grid and lines. Landscape orientation.

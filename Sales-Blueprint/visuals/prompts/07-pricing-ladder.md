# Visual Prompt: Pricing Ladder

**Purpose:** Shows a bounded, three-tier choice with one clear anchor, distinct from a wide menu of equally weighted options.

**Placement:** Module 7, Present the Offer, replacing `visuals/diagrams/figure-7.2-pricing-ladder.mmd`.

**Caption:** Figure 7.2: three tiers, one clearly anchored as the recommendation. A ladder, not a menu of equals.

**Generation prompt:**
A minimal vertical three-rung ladder diagram on a warm cream background (#FBF9F4). Three horizontal rounded rectangles stacked with visible gaps between them, connected by short vertical charcoal (#1B1F1E) connector lines suggesting rungs. Middle rung, labelled "Recommended," rendered larger and in deep teal (#0F3D3E) fill with cream text, visually the anchor. Top rung, "Enhanced," and bottom rung, "Reduced," both smaller and in pale teal (#E3EEEC) fill with charcoal text, visually subordinate to the middle. One short italic sans-serif subtitle per rung (enhanced: "recommended package plus a defined extra"; recommended: "solves the confirmed problem in full"; reduced: "a scaled-down version, named trade-offs"). Clean sans-serif typography, no gradients, no drop shadows. Portrait orientation.

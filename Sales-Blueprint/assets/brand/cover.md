## Brief used to generate it

Front cover only (no spine or back cover yet; those need a firm page count first, since spine width depends on it). Generated directly in Canva rather than as an external image-generation prompt, using the book's own palette rather than any Canva brand kit.

- **Title:** Sales Blueprint
- **Subtitle:** The Practical System for Attracting Customers, Closing More Sales and Building a Business People Trust
- **Author:** Samuel Omobusuyi
- **Publisher:** SSO Publishing
- **Proportions:** Portrait, roughly 1.6:1 height to width (approximating a 6x9 inch print trim)
- **Palette:** Warm cream background (`#FBF9F4`), deep teal accent (`#0F3D3E`), charcoal ink (`#1B1F1E`), per `BOOK_BIBLE.md` §7
- **Style:** Minimal, premium, business-consulting aesthetic; clean sans-serif typography, sentence case; no gradients, drop shadows, stock photography, or clip art
- **Graphic element:** A small row of connected rounded nodes in deep teal, a restrained nod to the Blueprint Framework's sequence, not a literal reproduction of Figure 0.1

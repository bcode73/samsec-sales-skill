# Worksheet 12: Digital Signal Audit

Use this on a lead or deal that started, or is running, primarily online or remotely.

## Step 1: Map your stages to digital equivalents

For each stage below, write the specific digital equivalent you're using right now, or the gap if you're not using one yet.

| Stage | In-person version | Your current digital equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Build Trust | Conversational competence signal | |
| Learn the Customer | Reading hesitation from body language | |
| Present the Offer | Handing across a physical proposal | |

## Step 2: Check your response time

How long did it take you to respond to this lead's first message? Would a same-day, specific reply have been possible? If not this time, what would make it possible next time?

## Step 3: Find the unasked question

Which discovery question from Module 4 have you been relying on reading from the room, that you should ask directly instead over video or in writing?

## Step 4: Make the offer shareable

Does your current proposal or offer exist as something the buyer can forward to a stakeholder who wasn't on the call? If not, build that version before your next digital deal reaches Module 7.

## Step 5: Apply it forward

Send the direct question from Step 3 in your next conversation with this lead, and note whether it surfaces something you wouldn't otherwise have learned.

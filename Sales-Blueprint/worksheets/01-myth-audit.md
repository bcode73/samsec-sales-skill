# Worksheet 1: Myth Audit

Use this with a real deal you lost in the last twelve months, one where you believed you had the stronger offer.

## Step 1: Write the polite reason

What did the buyer actually tell you, in their words or in the rejection email? Write it exactly as they said it.

## Step 2: Write the real reason

Guess honestly. If you asked them over a drink, off the record, what would they actually say? Common real reasons include: unsure you'd deliver on time, unsure you understood their specific situation, uneasy about a stakeholder who never got won over, or genuinely happy with an incumbent and never seriously shopping.

## Step 3: Match it to a myth

Which myth from this module was quietly shaping how you ran the deal?

- [ ] Myth one: I avoided being direct because I didn't want to seem pushy
- [ ] Myth two: I assumed my personality (or lack of a certain one) decided the outcome
- [ ] Myth three: I assumed the stronger product or lower price would win on its own
- [ ] Myth four: I treated an early hesitation as a fixed no and stopped probing

## Step 4: Name the actual gap

In one sentence, what would you need to have done differently, in what order, for the real reason in Step 2 to never have become a factor?

## Step 5: Apply it forward

Pick one open deal in your current pipeline. Using Step 4's answer, write down the single next action you'll take in the next 24 hours to close that same gap before it costs you this deal too.

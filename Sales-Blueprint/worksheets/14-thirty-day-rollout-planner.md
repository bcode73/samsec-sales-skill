# Worksheet 14: 30-Day Rollout Planner

Use this to plan your own rollout of the framework, week by week.

## Week 1: Build Trust and Learn the Customer

- Complete Worksheet 3 (trust audit) before your next new-prospect meeting.
- Ask at least one of Module 4's three discovery questions in every live conversation this week.
- By Friday, name the full buying committee on every open deal.

## Week 2: Understand the Problem and Establish Value

- Run Worksheet 5 (problem diagnosis) on your two or three most advanced open deals.
- Build a value stack (Worksheet 6) for each, with at least three itemised outcomes.
- By Friday, every deal you worked on has a buyer-confirmed diagnosis and value stack.

## Week 3: Present the Offer, Remove Doubt, Inspire Action

- Rebuild your proposal template using Worksheet 7: one recommendation, a scope boundary, a dated next step.
- Apply Worksheet 8 to any hesitation that surfaces this week.
- Apply Worksheet 9 to any deal where agreement hasn't yet turned into action.
- By Friday, at least one deal has moved from agreement to signed commitment using this sequence.

## Week 4: Nurture, Advocates, and Systems

- Pick three past customers you haven't spoken to since delivery and plan a genuine check-in (Worksheet 10) for each.
- Make one specific referral ask (Worksheet 11) with your most satisfied recent customer.
- Rebuild your pipeline around the nine Blueprint letters (Worksheet 13) and set your baseline metrics.

## Day 30 review

Answer honestly:

- Is every open deal correctly tagged to a real Blueprint stage?
- Does your proposal template lead with one recommendation, not a menu?
- Can you point to one resolved doubt and one shrunk next step from this month?
- Did you complete three genuine nurture conversations?
- Did you make at least one specific referral ask?

Whatever isn't yet true, repeat that week before moving on. The sequence matters more than the calendar.

# Worksheet 8: Objection Resolution

Use this the next time a live prospect hesitates or raises a concern.

## Step 1: Write down what they said

Use their exact words, not your interpretation.

## Step 2: Ask what's specifically behind it

If you haven't already, ask directly: "What specifically worries you about this?" Record the answer.

## Step 3: Sort the doubt

- [ ] Timing: will this disrupt something that matters to them
- [ ] Budget: will this cost more than agreed
- [ ] Delivery: will this actually get done as promised
- [ ] Internal buy-in: will someone else here object later

## Step 4: Check for specificity

Does the answer name a day, a number, a person, or a risk? If yes, it's a real, resolvable objection. If it's vague, an earlier stage (trust, discovery, or diagnosis) likely needs revisiting before this one can be resolved.

## Step 5: Build the resolution

Write the specific plan, document, or piece of evidence, not a general reassurance, that answers exactly what they named in Step 2.

## Step 6: Apply it forward

Deliver the resolution and note what happened next. If the deal moves forward, the doubt was real and now resolved. If a new hesitation appears, repeat this worksheet; it may be a different doubt underneath the first one.

# Template: Customer Persona

Introduced in Module 4 (Learn the Customer). Complete one of these per stakeholder in a buying committee, not one per deal; Module 2 covers why a single persona per deal usually misses the people who can actually block it.

## Role and title

## What they're personally measured on this year

From Module 4's second discovery question. Not the business goal; their individual stake in the outcome.

## What they'd find hard to defend internally

From Module 4's third discovery question. The concern most likely to surface later as an objection (see Module 8) if it isn't addressed early.

## How they prefer to communicate

In person, video, async message. Relevant for adapting your approach per Module 12.

## Their relationship to the budget

Do they control it, influence it, or answer for it afterward. Distinct roles that need different handling (see Module 2's buying committee).

## Trust signal most likely to matter to them

Competence, reliability, or candour (see Module 3's Trust Pyramid). Base this on what you learn about their past experience with similar purchases.

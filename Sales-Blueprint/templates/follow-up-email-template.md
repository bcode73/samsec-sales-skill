# Template: Nurture Follow-up Email

Introduced in Module 10 (Nurture Relationships). Use for a scheduled check-in, not a sales pitch. Before sending, apply the no-pitch test from Worksheet 10: if there were no possibility of future revenue, would you still send this?

## Six-week check-in

Subject: How's [the specific thing you delivered] working out?

Body: Reference the original diagnosed problem by name (not the product). Ask one specific question about whether it's actually solved. Nothing else. No mention of future work.

## Six-month check-in

Subject: Checking in

Body: Ask what's changed in their business since delivery. Genuine curiosity, not a lead-in to a pitch.

## Anniversary note

Subject: Thought of you

Body: Share something genuinely useful (an article, an observation from a comparable situation) with no ask attached.

## Referral ask (see Module 11)

Subject: A specific ask

Body: Name the specific person or type of contact you'd like an introduction to. State plainly why you're asking them specifically. One bounded action, not a general request to keep you in mind.

## Loop-closing note (after a referral)

Subject: Update on that introduction

Body: Report what happened, win or lose. This message matters more than the ask itself for whether the next referral happens.

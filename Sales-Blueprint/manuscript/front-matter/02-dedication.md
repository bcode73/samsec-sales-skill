# Dedication

For every business owner who is brilliant at the work and still dreads the sales call.

You didn't start your business because you love pitching. You started it because you're good at something and wanted to build a life around it. This book exists so the selling stops being the part you tolerate and starts being the part you understand.

And for the customers who said no, kindly or otherwise. You taught me more about this framework than any client who said yes ever did.

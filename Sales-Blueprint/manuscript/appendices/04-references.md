# References

The Blueprint Framework is an original synthesis, not a repackaging of another author's model, and no other book's framework, wording, or structure is reproduced here. Where a specific fact, quote, or figure would ordinarily need a citation, this book uses none because none of Kestrel Office Interiors' story is real: Priya Shah, Dominic, Marguerite, Tom, and every deal in this book are fictional, built to demonstrate the framework rather than to report on actual events.

## Further reading

If you want to go deeper into the ideas that sit alongside this book's approach, without treating any of them as this book's source material, the following are worth your time:

- *To Sell Is Human* by Daniel H. Pink
- *The Challenger Sale* by Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson
- *Never Split the Difference* by Chris Voss and Tahl Raz
- *Influence* by Robert Cialdini

Each takes a different angle on persuasion and selling than this book does; reading them alongside the Blueprint Framework will show you where the ideas agree, and where they don't.

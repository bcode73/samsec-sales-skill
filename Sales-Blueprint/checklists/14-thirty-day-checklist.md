# Checklist: The 30-Day Sales Blueprint

A quick-reference version of Module 14's rollout. Use Worksheet 14 for the detailed planning; use this to track completion week by week.

## Week 1: Build Trust and Learn the Customer

- [ ] Completed a trust audit (Worksheet 3) before a new-prospect meeting
- [ ] Asked at least one of the three discovery questions in every live conversation
- [ ] Named the full buying committee on every open deal

## Week 2: Understand the Problem and Establish Value

- [ ] Ran a problem diagnosis (Worksheet 5) on two or three advanced deals
- [ ] Built a value stack (Worksheet 6) with at least three itemised outcomes for each
- [ ] Confirmed each diagnosis with the buyer before proposing anything

## Week 3: Present the Offer, Remove Doubt, Inspire Action

- [ ] Rebuilt the proposal template around one recommendation, not a menu
- [ ] Named the scope boundary (what's included vs. a variation) in writing
- [ ] Resolved at least one specific, named doubt
- [ ] Shrunk at least one stalled next step and attached a genuine deadline
- [ ] Moved at least one deal from agreement to signed commitment

## Week 4: Nurture, Advocates, and Systems

- [ ] Completed three genuine, no-pitch check-ins with past customers
- [ ] Made one specific, named referral ask
- [ ] Closed the loop with that advocate, regardless of outcome
- [ ] Rebuilt the pipeline around the nine Blueprint letters
- [ ] Set a baseline for win rate, referral rate, and time to signature

## Day 30 review

- [ ] Every open deal is tagged to a real, honest Blueprint stage
- [ ] The proposal template leads with one recommendation
- [ ] There is at least one resolved doubt and one shrunk next step to point to
- [ ] Three nurture conversations and one referral ask are complete

If any box above is unchecked, repeat that week before starting the next cycle. The sequence matters more than the calendar.

**Long sales cycle?** Swap "moved at least one deal to signed commitment" for "correctly diagnosed, valued, and offered at least one deal using this sequence." The habit is what thirty days installs; the signature arrives on its own timeline.
